## 💡 Project Philosophy: Orderly Aesthetics & Cyber Refinement

**Modern Domain (摩域):** We believe that the best domains are the **shortest, most memorable, and most elegantly formed** 1-5 character prefixes. They represent the art of the digital age nameplate. When combined with a 2-5 character TLD suffix, the resulting domain, not exceeding 10 characters in total length, is defined as having the most **Modern** (摩登) attributes. This concept also indirectly pays tribute to the Zen Patriarch, Bodhidharma (达摩祖师).
We champion domain construction methods like those used by n8n, but we also support pure alphabetic, pure numeric, or mixed alphanumeric structures. Domains containing the hyphen symbol (`-`) are not recommended.

**Non-Profit Ethos:** We welcome all volunteers willing to contribute to automated domain querying and help expand this public project. While there are no service fees, we offer our sincerest acknowledgments on this page. We primarily offer manually curated, available short domains free of charge to those who need them. We recommend using Cloudflare's domain registration service but do not restrict the use of other registrars.

**Cyber Boddhisattva Chant:** This project is born out of gratitude for **Cyber Boddhisattvas** such as Cloudflare, Gemini, GitHub, and n8n, a profound understanding of the **scarcity of internet foundations (domains)**, and a pursuit of **automation and efficiency**. Utilizing the power of the **Cyber Boddhisattvas** and massive query registration (like **Chanting**), we overcome the technical hurdles of Whois/RDAP/various paid domain services to bring you the most accurate short domain list with "Modern Domain" value, aiming for equality among all domain seekers. **Self-Improvement Attribute:** You can refine your willpower and character through manual querying or quickly chant through automated querying.
